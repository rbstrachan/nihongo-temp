Navigating Japanese typography can be daunting, especially when trying to decipher varying handwriting and calligraphic styles for the first time. To assist you in your studies, we've compiled this reference to help you preview the 22 fonts available in the [Kotoba Discord bot](https://kotobaweb.com/), allowing you to observe how specific kanji radicals and strokes are displayed across different digital typefaces.

This page serves as a%%n interactive%% visual guide to help you choose the best font for your study sessions, giving you the opportunity to learn about and explore these styles beforehand.

# Selecting a Font in Kotoba
Type `k!settings`, then option <key>4</key> and option <key>1</key>. Finally type the number of the font you would like to use.
